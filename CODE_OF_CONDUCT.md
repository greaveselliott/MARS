# MARS Code of Conduct

## Our standard

MARS contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful, safe,
and constructive. Be considerate, critique ideas and code rather than people,
respect differing experience and identity, and accept good-faith moderation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, review,
community events, and other spaces where someone represents the MARS project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. If no private
channel is available, use the repository's **Request a private maintainer
contact** issue form without including incident or personal details; the
maintainer will arrange a private channel. The maintainer may remove content,
restrict participation, or ban an account when necessary to protect the
community. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical,
with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.
